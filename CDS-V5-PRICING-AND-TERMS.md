## Empowerment monthly website plans

These plans are for African founders, sole traders, community businesses, and small local enterprises that need a professional web presence but cannot comfortably pay the full build price upfront. They are **access plans**, not unlimited service plans and not charity pricing. The client receives a defined site, hosting support, and a predictable monthly payment.

| Plan | Price | Minimum term | Suitable for |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Empowerment One-Page | **R399/month** | 12 months | One landing page for a service, product, professional, or small business |
| Empowerment Presence | **R699/month** | 12 months | Up to three pages with contact, services, about, or gallery content |
| Empowerment Business | **R999/month** | 12 months | Up to five pages, stronger local visibility, and a managed enquiry path |

### Included in all Empowerment plans

- Responsive website hosted on the agreed hosting platform
- SSL where supported by the hosting platform
- One agreed domain connection
- Basic page metadata and social sharing image setup
- Supplied logo, photographs, and final client-approved text
- One small monthly content update within the plan allowance
- Basic support through the agreed communication channel
- Website ownership and handover terms stated clearly in the agreement

### Not automatically included

- Domain registration or renewal fees
- Premium hosting upgrades
- Paid email accounts
- Paid plugins, API fees, stock images, fonts, or third-party services
- Ecommerce, bookings, payments, memberships, databases, CRM, AI agents, dashboards, or custom integrations
- New page design, a full redesign, extensive copywriting, photo editing, video production, or social-media management
- Unlimited edits, unlimited storage, or unlimited support

### Empowerment ownership rule

The simplest customer-friendly structure is:

> The website is built and maintained under the monthly plan. After the 12-month minimum term is complete and all invoices are paid, the client may continue monthly care, request a paid handover, or move to a standard once-off ownership arrangement. Third-party accounts, domain fees, hosting subscriptions, and licensed assets remain subject to their own provider terms.

Do not use the phrase “you own everything” until the contract defines the treatment of source code, design assets, domain registration, hosting, paid licences, and outstanding invoices.

## Payment and project conditions wording

### Pricing and scope

> Prices shown are starting prices for the stated package scope. The final price depends on the agreed pages, content, assets, integrations, migration requirements, and approval timeline. Work outside the stated scope is quoted separately before it begins.

### Deposit and commencement

> Once-off website projects begin after a 50% deposit has cleared. The remaining 50% is due before final handover or production launch. For larger projects, an alternative milestone schedule may be agreed in writing.

### Content and assets

> The client supplies or approves the final business information, text, logo, photographs, contact details, prices, legal wording, and other public claims. The client confirms that supplied assets may be used for the project. Caramel Digital Studio does not invent reviews, credentials, locations, prices, results, registrations, service areas, or partner claims.

### Revisions

> A revision round means one consolidated set of changes returned together. The included number of rounds applies to the agreed scope and visual direction. New pages, new functionality, a changed business direction, or replacement content after approval may be quoted separately.

### Third-party services

> Domain registration, hosting upgrades, email, payment providers, form services, stock media, fonts, APIs, plugins, analytics services, and other third-party costs are not included unless the proposal says so. Where a third-party service is required, the client remains responsible for the account, subscription, terms, and ongoing cost unless a separate management arrangement is agreed.

### Timeline and approvals

> Delivery timing depends on receiving complete content, assets, access, decisions, and approvals. Delayed feedback or missing materials can move the delivery date. A preview is for review; production release happens only after the client approves the agreed content and functionality.

### Hosting and maintenance

> Monthly care covers only the updates and checks listed in the selected plan. It does not include unlimited edits, unlimited support, major redesigns, new pages, new integrations, or third-party subscription costs. Unused monthly allowances do not roll over unless agreed in writing.

### Empowerment plans

> Empowerment plans use a defined monthly scope and a 12-month minimum term. The monthly payment covers the agreed website access and support described on the plan. It does not include unlimited content, ecommerce, custom software, paid third-party services, or unlimited revisions. Handover, cancellation, late payment, domain ownership, hosting, and licensed assets are governed by the written agreement.

### Cancellation and non-payment

> If an invoice remains unpaid, work and support may pause until the account is brought up to date. Cancellation, suspension, handover, refunds, and access to hosted services are handled according to the written agreement and applicable law. Caramel Digital Studio will not remove a client’s domain or public website without following the agreed process.

### Results and SEO

> SEO, analytics, visibility, enquiries, and business outcomes depend on the client’s market, offer, competition, content, approvals, technical environment, and many external factors. Caramel Digital Studio provides defined work and reporting where included; it does not guarantee rankings, traffic, sales, reviews, or revenue.

### Privacy and communications

> Contact and enquiry information is used for the agreed project communication and service delivery. The applicable privacy notice explains what information is collected, where it is sent, how long it is retained, and which providers process it.
