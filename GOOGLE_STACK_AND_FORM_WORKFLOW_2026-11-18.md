# Google stack and enquiry-form workflow — 2026-11-18

## Current enquiry form

The live Caramel static site uses Web3Forms for the project enquiry form in `start.html`.

The current workflow:

1. Visitor completes the Caramel project form.
2. Web3Forms receives the submission.
3. The message reaches the connected Caramel inbox.
4. The page displays the submission status.
5. WhatsApp and direct email remain available as additional contact routes.

The Web3Forms workflow stays in place while the Google booking and Workspace layers are built.

## Recommended workflow for Caramel consultations

1. Visitor opens the Caramel 05A Digital Marketing Guidance section.
2. Visitor chooses a Google Calendar appointment type.
3. Google Calendar collects the booking details and appointment time.
4. Google Forms supplies a deeper preparation form when the consultation requires research.
5. Responses enter a designated Drive folder and Sheet register.
6. Gmail sends the preparation request and appointment guidance.
7. Google Meet hosts the consultation.
8. Gemini helps prepare a summary from approved information.
9. The strategy summary saves into the relevant Silo A or Silo B folder.
10. The agent updates the repository handoff log and GitHub Issue.
