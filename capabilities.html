03 · Scaling

Scaling to SaaS

Every Caramel Digital Studio site is architected to grow. A brochure site or service presence scales directly into a fully operational SaaS platform. The transition is a seamless activation of the existing foundation.

  • Moving from a static site to a live application layer
  • User authentication: registration, login, roles, and permissions
  • Subscription and payment processing built directly into the site
  • Automated onboarding flows for new users or clients
  • API integrations: connect your site to any tool, database, or service
  • Multi-tenant architecture: serve many clients or users from one platform
  • Usage-based billing, tiered access, and feature gating
  • Admin controls, user dashboards, and real-time data
  • Automated workflows replacing manual processes entirely
  • AI agents handling customer support, lead qualification, onboarding, and operations
