# How to pay for your Caramel Digital Studio consultation

Caramel Digital Studio offers a choice of payment methods. Choose the option that suits your country, currency, account, and preference.

Your consultation booking receives confirmation after payment receipt has been checked.

## South African EFT to Capitec

Choose South African EFT when you prefer a direct local bank payment.

1. Reply to your consultation email and choose **South African EFT**.
2. Caramel Digital Studio sends the current Capitec payment instructions privately.
3. Pay the exact consultation amount in South African rand.
4. Reply with your payment reference or a payment confirmation screenshot with sensitive account details covered.
5. The owner checks receipt and sends your Google Calendar and Google Meet confirmation.

The private payment email contains the account details required for the transfer. Please use those current details for your payment.

## Mukuru

Choose Mukuru when it suits your country and transfer route.

1. Reply to your consultation email and choose **Mukuru**.
2. Caramel Digital Studio confirms the available Mukuru route and sends the current receiving instructions privately.
3. Pay the exact consultation amount using the selected Mukuru route.
4. Reply with your payment reference or payment confirmation.
5. The owner checks receipt and sends your Google Calendar and Google Meet confirmation.

Mukuru availability, fees, limits, and delivery times depend on the selected route. Caramel Digital Studio confirms the available method before you pay.

## Wise

Choose Wise when you prefer an international payment method or already use Wise.

Depending on your country and the owner’s current Wise setup, the available Wise method may include:

- a Wise payment link;
- a Wise payment request;
- Wise account details for a bank transfer;
- searching for the recipient by an approved Wise email or Wise account route;
- a Wisetag link or QR code;
- card payment where the Wise payment link enables it;
- Google Pay where the Wise payment route enables it;
- another approved Wise receiving method.

### Wise payment steps

1. Reply to your consultation email and choose **Wise**.
2. Caramel Digital Studio confirms the available Wise method for your country and payment preference.
3. If you choose to use Wise, open or access your Wise account through the owner’s supplied referral link when provided:

   `[OWNER_WISE_REFERRAL_LINK]`

4. Pay the exact consultation amount using the private Wise instructions.
5. Reply with your Wise payment reference or payment confirmation.
6. The owner checks receipt and sends your Google Calendar and Google Meet confirmation.

Wise fees, currencies, payment methods, account details, and delivery times depend on your location and the Wise route selected. Caramel Digital Studio confirms the available option before payment.

## Europe and United States

Wise can support international receiving methods such as EUR account details, SEPA-style transfers where available, USD account details, ACH or domestic wire options where available, payment links, and payment requests. The available method depends on the owner’s Wise account and your location.

Tell us your country and preferred method. We will send the matching payment instructions.

## Payment confirmation

Please send only the payment reference or a screenshot with sensitive account details covered. The owner checks receipt privately. Calendar and Meet details follow receipt confirmation.

## Additional work

Your consultation covers the agreed session and preparation. Additional work receives a separate written quote before work begins.
