# Caramel Digital Studio payment methods launch guidelines

**Status:** Approved launch-phase direction for agent work.

## Launch decision

PayPal sits outside the current launch phase. The current consultation flow uses a client-choice payment menu and owner-confirmed receipt.

The menu presents the methods that suit the client’s country, currency, account access, and preference. The agent asks the client which method they prefer and sends the matching private instructions.

## Core payment methods

### South African EFT to Capitec

Use Capitec as the official bank name. Offer South African EFT for clients who prefer a direct local bank payment. The owner supplies the private account instructions and checks receipt in the owner’s banking channel.

### Mukuru

Offer Mukuru where it suits the client’s country and transfer route. The owner supplies the current receiving instructions and confirms the payment through the owner’s private record. Availability, fees, limits, and delivery time depend on the selected Mukuru route.

### Wise

Offer Wise for international clients and for clients who prefer Wise. Depending on the owner’s Wise setup and the client’s location, the available method may include a Wise payment link, payment request, Wise account details, Wise email search, Wisetag link or QR code, card payment, or another Wise-supported method.

Wise payment links can support a single-use or reusable request, an amount, a currency, a service description, and payment methods enabled in the owner’s account. The agent checks the current Wise account before sending a method to a client.

When a client chooses to create or use a Wise account through an onboarding route, the agent uses the owner-provided Wise referral link. Repository placeholder:

`[OWNER_WISE_REFERRAL_LINK]`

Replace the placeholder only after Melissa supplies the final referral URL. Never invent or publish a referral URL.

## Europe and US discovery options

Wise remains the primary international option for launch discovery. Agents can record the following optional routes for later owner review:

- Europe: EUR Wise account details and SEPA transfer where available in the owner’s account.
- United States: USD Wise account details for ACH or domestic wire where available in the owner’s account.
- International: Wise payment link, card payment, Wise request, account details, Wisetag, or international transfer where available.
- Other local methods: add only after the owner confirms the account, fees, reconciliation process, and client demand.

The agent presents options rather than promising every method for every client. The owner checks current Wise eligibility, receiving currencies, fees, and account capabilities before publication.

## Client-choice sequence

1. Ask for the client’s country.
2. Ask which available method they prefer.
3. Present the relevant options.
4. Send the selected method’s private instructions.
5. Match the payment reference to the consultation.
6. Check receipt in the owner’s private payment channel.
7. Confirm the appointment after receipt verification.
8. Send Calendar, Meet, and preparation details.

## Privacy and records

Keep bank details, payment links, Wise identifiers, Wisetag links, QR codes, access credentials, referral URLs, and private account information out of public source, GitHub Issues, and general handover files. Store them in the owner’s approved private record.
