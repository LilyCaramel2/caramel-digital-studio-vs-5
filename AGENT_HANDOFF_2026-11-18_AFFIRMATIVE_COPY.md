# Writing rule — 2026-11-18

Caramel Digital Studio content uses affirmative, direct language.

Describe each service through its purpose, deliverables, process, value, audience, timing, and next step. Use plain sentences and positive descriptions.

Write:

- “The package includes four SEO-led content updates.”
- “The consultation gives the client a focused marketing direction.”
- “Stage 1 creates a polished static foundation.”
- “Stage 3 connects the approved workflows, database, dashboard, and integration.”

Use clear scope, revision, client-input, payment, and timeline wording in their own direct sentences. Place practical details where clients can find them quickly.

Avoid negative comparisons and exclusion-led sales copy. Keep each service page focused on what the client receives and how Caramel delivers it.

This rule applies to website copy, pricing pages, proposals, decks, Markdown handoffs, GitHub Issues, emails, and agent summaries.
