# Google Calendar and Caramel booking workflow
## Presentation outline for Gemini and future agents

### Slide 1 — One connected Caramel workspace

Caramel Digital Studio uses Google Workspace as the operating environment for communication, documents, appointments, meetings, and business knowledge.

Visual: Google Workspace flow from Gmail to Drive to Calendar to Meet to Gemini.

### Slide 2 — The account foundation

Primary workspace account:

`carameldigitalcontent@gmail.com`

This account connects the Google Calendar, Drive, Google One storage, Gmail, Meet, and Gemini workflow.

Visual: one account hub with the connected Workspace tools around it.

### Slide 3 — Silo A: core business systems

Silo A holds the operating records for:

- Caramel Digital Studio
- Learn AI to Earn
- High Tide Tours
- Cape Plaster and Pools

Visual: Drive folder tree with `00 Agent Logs`, the four business folders, shared operations, Gemini instructions, client workflows, and archive.

### Slide 4 — Silo B: demos and client builds

Silo B holds demonstration builds, client projects, experiments, previews, and delivery assets.

Visual: separate Drive folder tree with a clear boundary between core business systems and client-build work.

### Slide 5 — Agent continuity

Every agent reads the repository handoff log, records the work completed, updates the GitHub Issue, and stores approved supporting records in the Silo A agent-log folder.

Visual: agent → Markdown handoff → GitHub Issue → Drive log.

### Slide 6 — Google Calendar Appointment Schedules

Create four appointment types:

- Free project-fit conversation — 30 minutes
- Digital Marketing Consultation — 60 minutes — R950
- Digital Marketing Strategy Session — 90 minutes — R1,350
- Advisory Session — 60 minutes — R900 per session within the R2,700 block

Visual: four appointment cards using the Caramel colour system.

### Slide 7 — The booking experience

1. Visitor chooses a Caramel appointment.
2. Google Calendar shows available times.
3. Visitor shares the business priority and contact details.
4. Calendar creates the appointment.
5. Google Meet provides the meeting room.
6. Gmail sends the confirmation and preparation details.
7. The owner receives the booking in Calendar and Gmail.

Visual: booking journey from website button to confirmed Meet appointment.

### Slide 8 — 05A Digital Marketing Guidance

The 05A section sits after Business Add-ons and before Brand Direction on the locked pricing page.

It presents direct guidance for SEO, long-form writing, lead generation, website growth, social direction, analytics, advertising, booking, CRM planning, and connected systems.

Visual: native Caramel pricing cards with the four appointment types.

### Slide 9 — Paid consultations and Stripe

Paid appointment schedules connect Google Calendar to Stripe. The client chooses a time, completes payment, and receives the calendar invitation and meeting details.

The owner confirms the Workspace plan, Stripe account, South African rand settings, cancellation policy, and refund workflow during setup.

Visual: Calendar → Stripe → Gmail → Meet.

### Slide 10 — Gemini as the Workspace assistant

Gemini supports approved business information across Gmail, Docs, Drive, and Meet. The Silo A `Gemini Instructions` folder gives the assistant clear business context, brand language, offer structure, agent rules, and log conventions.

Visual: Gemini connected to approved Drive folders and business workflows.

### Slide 11 — The growth path

Stage 1 creates a focused public foundation and lead-generation route.

Stage 2 improves content, SEO, visibility, measurement, and conversion.

Stage 3 connects the approved workflows, database, dashboard, booking, CRM, and integrations.

Stage 4 and Stage 5 expand into intelligent operations, advanced AI, portals, and larger systems.

Visual: five-stage staircase using the existing Caramel palette.

### Slide 12 — Immediate setup sequence

- Confirm the Google Workspace account.
- Create the Drive Silo A and Silo B folders.
- Create the four Calendar Appointment Schedules.
- Connect Google Meet.
- Connect Stripe for paid schedules.
- Add intake questions and preparation guidance.
- Place the 05A HTML section on the pricing page review branch.
- Add the final Google Calendar URLs.
- Test desktop and mobile booking journeys.
- Record the final configuration in Drive and GitHub Issue #1.
