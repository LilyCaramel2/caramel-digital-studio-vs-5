08 · client terms

Clear terms. Better work.

Scope and extras

Prices are starting prices for the stated scope. New pages, new features, changed direction, extensive content, image production, integrations, ecommerce, booking, CRM, AI workflows, or work outside the agreed scope are quoted before they begin.

Client content and approvals

The client supplies or approves public business information, copy, images, logos, prices, contact details, and legal wording. Caramel Digital Studio does not invent reviews, credentials, locations, registrations, outcomes, or partner claims.

Revisions

A revision round means one consolidated set of changes returned together. The included rounds apply to the agreed direction. A new direction after approval may be quoted separately.

Third-party services

Domain registration, email, hosting upgrades, form services, stock media, fonts, APIs, plugins, analytics, and other third-party costs are separate unless the proposal says otherwise.

Launch and handover

A preview may be ready before final payment. Production publication, domain connection, final file release, source handover, and transfer of managed services remain subject to payment of all amounts due.

Results

SEO, traffic, enquiries, reviews, sales, and revenue depend on the market, offer, competition, content, approvals, and external platforms. Defined work can be delivered; outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Payment summary: Small sites use 50% to begin and 50% before launch or final file release. Larger builds may use 40% to begin, 40% after design and core content approval, and 20% before launch or handover. Care Plans begin with the setup fee and first monthly payment, with a six-month initial term.
